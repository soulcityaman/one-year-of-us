ONE YEAR TOGETHER — DIGITAL SCRAPBOOK

This version includes all 40 photos supplied for the anniversary scrapbook.

EDITING:
1. Open index.html.
2. Find the section marked EASY EDIT AREA / SCRAPBOOK.
3. Change text, captions, dates, and image filenames there.
4. Your photos are in assets/photos/ as photo-01.jpg through photo-40.jpg.
5. Music and album art remain in assets/.

PHOTO NOTE:
The supplied HEIC photos were converted to web-friendly JPG copies so Safari/iPhone hosting works without relying on HEIC browser support.

NAVIGATION:
• Left click/tap: previous p
• Right click: next page
• Swipe left/right: next/previous
• Arrow keys: previous/next
• Tap the mini music player: immersive player + lyrics

The scrapbook is designed portrait-first for an iPhone 14 Pro and adapts to wider screens.
